✨ How it works

1
Snap your receipt

After shopping, take a photo of the receipt – long receipts in several parts. E-receipts as PDF work too.

2
NomBon reads it all

Store, date, every product and every price – sorted into fruit, vegetables, meat, sweets and more.

3
See it clearly

What does our shopping cost per month? What is getting pricier? Where is it cheaper? What do we actually eat?

🧾 What NomBon shows you

  • Spending per month, with your own budget if you like
  • Price history of your products – and which ones got pricier
  • Store comparison: where your products are cheaper
  • Your most frequent products and categories
  • Roughly estimated nutrition per person and day
  • Monthly review to look at and share

You can add old receipts later, so you can compare prices over several months.

🔒 Your data stays with you

No account, no sign-up. Your purchases stay on your phone. Only the receipt photo is briefly sent to our server to be read, and it is not stored afterwards. No tracking, no ads.
