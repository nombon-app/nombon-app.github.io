2. Basic principle

NomBon does not need an account. There is no advertising, no tracking, no analytics tools and no sale of data.

3. Your purchases stay on your device

NomBon stores the recognised receipts (store, date, items, prices, estimated nutrition values) and your household settings only locally on your phone. We have no access to them. If you delete a receipt or the app, the data is gone. Depending on your phone’s settings, they may be included in your own device backup (iCloud or Google).

4. Reading the receipts

When you photograph a receipt, choose a photo or import an e-receipt (PDF), that image or file is sent in encrypted form to our server and read there by an AI. The result (the item list) is sent back to your phone. The photo or file is not stored by us. Receipts may contain details such as the payment method, parts of a card number or loyalty-card customer numbers; these are neither evaluated nor stored.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you use the function to have your receipt read).

Abbreviation dictionary

So that abbreviations on receipts (e.g. “LakFr H-Milch”) are recognised better, our server remembers only the following for individual item lines: the retail chain, the text of the line without the price, and the recognised product name with its category. Not stored are photos, prices, date, time, branch, payment data or which items you bought together. Unknown lines are resolved once by an AI – as text only.

If you correct a product name in the app, your phone remembers the correction. In addition, it is sent to the server without any personal reference (chain, line text, product name, category and a random identifier of your app installation, so that a correction counts only once). Only when several installations correct the same thing does it apply to everyone. Lines containing customer or card numbers are never adopted. Entries that have not appeared for 18 months are deleted.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in better recognition; the data does not allow any conclusions about you).

5. Service providers

  • Server: Supabase Inc., data centre in Frankfurt am Main (EU), with a data processing agreement. When sending, technically necessary data such as your IP address is processed briefly.
  • AI for reading the photos: OpenAI, L.L.C. (USA), via its API. According to OpenAI, data sent through this interface is not used for training; we have switched off the storage of requests. The transfer to the USA is based on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • AI for the abbreviation dictionary (text only): Anthropic PBC (USA), Claude model, via its API. According to Anthropic, the data is not used for training. The basis is likewise standard contractual clauses.
  • App delivery and updates: Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), as well as the Apple App Store and Google Play.
  • This website: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).

6. Permissions

Camera: only for photographing your receipts. Photos/files: only the images or PDFs that you select yourself.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since we store no data about you, this mainly concerns the brief processing when a receipt is read. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Nutrition values

The nutrition values shown are rough estimates and not nutritional or health advice.
